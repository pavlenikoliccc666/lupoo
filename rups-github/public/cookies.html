Cookie Policy

What this website stores on your device, and how you can control it.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

On this page

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies, including local storage and tracking pixels, may also store information or help a service recognise a visit.

Our website’s own code

After admin sign-in, the essential __Host-lupoo_admin cookie keeps the administrator signed in for up to eight hours. It contains a random session identifier, is restricted to this website, and is marked Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict. Signing out invalidates the session and clears the cookie. It is not used for advertising.

Rups’s website code does not currently set analytics, advertising or preference cookies. It does not use tracking pixels or save your demo form details in local storage. Navigation, animations and the enquiry form do not require us to remember you across visits.

Hosting and access controls

The site is hosted through Sites. The hosting service may use technologies needed for delivery, security or access control. In a private review version, the hosting provider may require sign-in and use authentication cookies. These are distinct from cookies set by Rups’s website code.

For information about the hosting provider’s practices, see OpenAI’s Cookie Policy.

Forms and social media

Demo requests are saved directly through this website. The public form does not set a cookie or save contact details in browser storage.

Social profile links are ordinary links, not embedded feeds or tracking widgets. Visiting an external profile may allow that platform to set cookies under its own policy.

Your controls

You can view, delete or block cookies through your browser’s privacy settings. Blocking cookies required for sign-in or verification may prevent those external services from working.

No optional analytics or advertising is enabled by Rups’s website code, so there are no optional tracking categories to switch on here. If that changes, this policy and any required choices will need to be updated before the new technologies are used.

More information

Read our Privacy Policy for how enquiries are handled. For cookie-related questions, email privacy@rups.io.
