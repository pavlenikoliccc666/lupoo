A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING.

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What can Rups do for my dental clinic?

Rups helps with appointment enquiries, booking, patient follow-ups and frequently asked questions. The goal is to turn more enquiries into appointments and give your team time back.

Which channels does Rups work on?

Rups works on WhatsApp, Instagram and Viber, so patients can reach your clinic through the channels they already use. We can also connect your clinic’s phone number to supported messaging channels. We’ll confirm the setup for your accounts and number during your demo.

Can Rups handle phone calls?

Coming soon: we’re adding an AI calling system for your clinic. Voice calls are not available yet; we’ll share availability and setup details when the calling system launches.

Does it work outside our opening hours?

Yes. Rups’s AI agents can handle routine patient conversations 24/7, including evenings and weekends.

How does Rups know about our clinic?

Its answers are grounded in information you provide and approve, using RAG. We review your services, policies and common questions so the experience reflects your clinic.

Will it replace our reception team?

Rups is there to support your team with repetitive administration and after-hours enquiries. Your people remain essential for personal care, complex requests and situations that need human judgment.

Can it give patients dental advice?

Rups’s role is administrative. It should refer clinical questions and treatment decisions to a qualified member of your dental team.

Can it work with our booking system?

We’ll review your current booking system during the demo and confirm what can be connected before agreeing on your setup.

What happens during the demo?

Leave your contact details and our team will get in touch to arrange a personal walkthrough. We’ll explore your clinic’s needs and show examples of booking and follow-up conversations.
