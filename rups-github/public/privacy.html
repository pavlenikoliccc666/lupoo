Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you visit Rups’s website or ask us for a demo.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

On this page

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Rups website and enquiries made through it. Rups is the business presenting this website. You can contact us about personal information at privacy@rups.io.

It does not describe the processing of patient records within a dental clinic. Any use of Rups services involving clinic or patient data needs a separate agreement and appropriate data-processing arrangements.

Information you provide

Our demo request form collects your name, work email, clinic name, area of interest and an optional phone number. If you email us, we also receive the content of your message and any information you choose to include.

Please provide business contact details only. Do not include patient records, medical information, payment card details or other sensitive information in the enquiry form.

Why we use your information

We use enquiry information to respond to you, understand your clinic’s needs, arrange a demo and maintain relevant business correspondence. A demo request does not subscribe you to a newsletter or create a service contract.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, responding to an enquiry may involve steps you request before entering a contract, or our legitimate interest in handling business enquiries. Where consent is required for a separate activity, it must be obtained for that activity.

Enquiry storage and service providers

Demo requests are sent to this website and saved in its hosted database. Rups accesses them through a password-protected admin area to respond to your enquiry. The form does not automatically send an email or book a meeting. If you contact enquiries@rups.io directly, email providers handle that correspondence.

The website’s hosting, database and security providers process information to run and protect the site. Security controls use a hashed identifier derived from an IP address for temporary request limits. Hosting providers may also process IP addresses and request logs. Provider processing may take place outside your country; contact us for details relevant to your enquiry.

Cookies and website storage

The website’s own code does not set advertising or analytics cookies, use tracking pixels, or save form details in browser storage. The admin area uses an essential sign-in cookie. Hosting and access controls may use their own technologies. See our Cookie Policy for more information.

Retention and security

Enquiry information should be kept only for as long as needed to handle the enquiry, maintain relevant business records, and meet applicable legal obligations. The appropriate period depends on the nature of the correspondence. Contact us to ask about or request deletion of a specific enquiry.

The form uses HTTPS on the hosted website. Admin access requires a password, and passwords are stored as hashes. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not send sensitive patient information through this website.

Your choices and rights

You can choose not to submit the form and contact us directly instead. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a request, email privacy@rups.io. We may need information to verify the request before acting. You may also be entitled to complain to the data-protection authority responsible for your location.

External links and changes

Links to social media and other websites take you to services operated by third parties. Their privacy policies apply when you use them. We may update this policy as the site or its services change; the date above identifies the latest version.

Let’s keep it clear.

For questions about this policy, contact privacy@rups.io.
