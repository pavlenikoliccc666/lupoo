Privacy Policy

How Rups handles website enquiries, protects information and prepares its online software for dental clinics.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Scope and contact

Rups is an independently operated project based in Montenegro. Our correspondence address is Gavra Vukovića bb, 81000 Podgorica, Montenegro. For personal-data questions or requests, contact privacy@rups.io; for service enquiries, contact support@rups.io.

This policy explains the current website and demo-enquiry process. The Rups online software service (SaaS) for dental clinics is still under development; it is not a downloadable application. Sections describing that software explain its intended use and the arrangements needed before it starts processing clinic information. They do not represent a completed deployment or a certification of compliance.

The date above identifies this version. This notice should be read with our Cookie Policy and Terms of Service.

Information collected through the website

The demo form collects your name, work email address, clinic name, area of interest and, if you choose to provide it, phone number. We also record the submission and verification times. The required fields allow us to verify and respond to your request; the phone number is optional. You can contact us by email instead of using the form.

If you email us, we receive the message and information you include. Please send business contact information only. Do not put patient records, symptoms, medical attachments, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information in a demo request.

Hosting and email delivery can involve technical information such as IP addresses, request times, browser information and delivery or error logs. Our server also uses temporary hashed identifiers derived from IP addresses and email addresses to limit repeated requests and protect the form. Hashing these identifiers does not necessarily make them anonymous.

Purposes and legal grounds

We use enquiry information to verify that you control the email address, respond to your request, understand your clinic’s needs, arrange a demonstration and maintain related correspondence. A verified email does not automatically book a meeting or start a paid subscription.

Where GDPR applies, responding to a business representative normally relies on our legitimate interest in managing business enquiries. Steps requested by an individual before entering a contract may instead rely on the pre-contractual basis where that basis is applicable. Fraud prevention and service security serve our legitimate interest in protecting the website and its users. Records required by an applicable law may be retained to meet that obligation.

Submitting an enquiry is not consent to unrelated marketing or to the processing of patient health information. Any separate marketing activity requires an appropriate legal basis and any consent required by the applicable rules.

Email verification and enquiry storage

Vercel hosts the website and server endpoints. Supabase stores pending verification details, confirmed demo enquiries and administrative authentication records. Resend delivers the verification message to the address you enter. Authorised Rups administration uses the enquiry records to respond to you.

Codes are valid for ten minutes, and a pending challenge allows up to five code attempts. Successful verification removes the pending record and creates a confirmed enquiry. Expired pending records are cleaned up during subsequent rate-limited activity; expiry does not mean every stored copy is erased at that exact moment. If sending the code fails, the website attempts to remove the associated pending record.

The public website does not offer a patient-record upload facility. Demo information submitted through this website is not sent to an AI model by the demo-form code.

Providers, disclosures and international processing

The website depends on hosting, database and email-delivery providers. They receive the information needed for their respective functions. Their current privacy information is available from Vercel, Supabase and Resend. These links provide additional information and do not replace Rups’s responsibilities.

Information may be processed outside your country through provider infrastructure or support. This policy does not promise that all information stays in Montenegro or the European Economic Area. Contact us for the processing locations and transfer arrangements relevant to your enquiry. Where a transfer is subject to legal safeguards, those safeguards must be established; this notice alone is not a transfer agreement.

We may disclose information where required by a valid legal obligation, to respond to a lawful request, or where necessary and legally permitted to establish or defend legal claims. We do not sell demo enquiries or provide them to advertisers. Social-media links lead to separately operated services with their own privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Confirmed demo enquiries do not currently have a fixed automatic deletion timer. Retention depends on whether the enquiry is still active, whether follow-up is reasonably needed, and whether particular records must be kept for an applicable legal obligation or a dispute. A completed or abandoned enquiry should not be kept indefinitely merely because storage is available.

You can ask us to review, correct or delete an enquiry by contacting privacy@rups.io. If a legal exception requires retaining particular information, the response should explain the applicable reason. Deleting an active record does not necessarily immediately remove provider logs or backup copies, which have separate retention arrangements.

The planned clinic software’s retention schedule has not yet been finalised. No three-month or six-month automatic deletion promise is currently made. Before activation, retention periods or meaningful criteria, deletion responsibilities and backup handling must be specified for the clinic’s actual workflow.

Security

The hosted website uses HTTPS. Administrative access requires authentication, passwords are stored as hashes rather than plain text, and session cookies use security attributes. The enquiry database schema restricts access to the server’s database role. Request limits help reduce repeated submissions and login attempts.

These measures describe the current website; they are not a security audit of the future SaaS. No online service can promise complete security. Please report a suspected privacy or security problem to privacy@rups.io without sending passwords, verification codes or patient records. Applicable incident-notification duties remain in force.

Planned clinic software and patient information

Rups is being developed to help clinics handle appointment enquiries, booking workflows, follow-ups and frequently asked questions. Planned channels include WhatsApp, Instagram and a clinic phone-number integration. The available channel configuration will be agreed with each clinic.

PostgreSQL is the planned database technology and OpenAI is the planned AI provider. A database technology is not itself a hosting location, retention policy or security guarantee. The final hosting providers, fields, access permissions, AI configuration and retention arrangements must be documented before real patient data is used.

The software is not intended to maintain clinical medical records or retain call recordings. Nevertheless, a message or appointment detail can reveal health information. Administrative use does not automatically remove that information from applicable health-data protections. Patients should avoid unnecessary sensitive details and direct clinical matters to the clinic.

When Rups processes patient information only on a clinic’s documented instructions, the clinic normally determines why it is processed and Rups acts as a processor/service provider. The clinic’s own privacy notice remains relevant. Actual roles and processing duties must be recorded in a suitable customer agreement and, where required, a data processing agreement (DPA).

AI, healthcare rules and human oversight

The planned assistant may use clinic-provided information to help generate responses. Grounding an AI answer in that information does not eliminate mistakes. Clinical advice, emergency situations and treatment decisions require qualified human handling.

AI data flows, provider retention settings, any model-improvement use and human review must be disclosed for the finished service. This website does not promise zero provider retention or claim that every future configuration will have identical privacy settings. The current demo form is not used to make solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about applicants.

GDPR and HIPAA apply according to the relevant law, data and parties’ roles. This policy is not a HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, a Business Associate Agreement (BAA), or a claim of HIPAA certification. HIPAA-regulated use requires assessment of the actual workflow, appropriate safeguards and required BAAs, including relevant downstream providers. The DPA and BAA do not replace one another or waive statutory duties.

Your rights and complaints

Depending on the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. Some rights depend on the circumstances and are subject to lawful exceptions.

Send requests about Rups’s website processing to privacy@rups.io, identifying the enquiry or information concerned. We may request proportionate information to verify identity or authority, rather than collecting unnecessary identification documents. Requests must be handled within the period required by the applicable law.

For information controlled by a clinic, contact the clinic first; a service provider must assist within its role and applicable duties. You may complain to Montenegro’s Agency for Personal Data Protection and Free Access to Information, or to another competent supervisory authority where the applicable law permits. You do not have to waive this right or finish an internal complaint process first.

Children, cookies and policy changes

The website’s demo form is intended for clinic representatives and business users, not for children requesting care. This does not mean that future clinic workflows can never involve children’s information; any such workflow requires the appropriate notices, legal basis and guardian arrangements where applicable.

The website’s own code does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. An essential cookie supports administrator sign-in. The Cookie Policy explains this and distinguishes browser storage from records held on the server.

We may update this policy when the website, software or processing changes. Material changes require appropriate notice. The future software notice will need to be updated before new processing begins; a changed policy cannot retrospectively authorise an incompatible use of personal information.
